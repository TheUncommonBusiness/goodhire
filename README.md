# GoodHire

**The professional network for dogs.** Every adoptable dog at Austin Pets Alive! (APA), written up like the best candidate you'll ever interview, so you can "hire" one as your next family member.

Built overnight with AI for UCL Live Austin (November 2026) by The Uncommon Business.

## Pages
- `/` the app: feed, network, dog profiles, your recruit, alerts
- `/stage.html` the stage show: hype reel (Space to play, S to skip, R to replay), then three live iPhones
- `/qr.html` a full-screen QR code for the room

## Where the data comes from
- Dogs, photos, bios, tags and videos: APA's public adoption listings (Shelterluv shelter 464), pulled politely.
- Profile copy: written by AI from those facts only. No invented compatibility, health or behavior claims, and no people's names.
- Headshots: AI-generated "professional headshots" of the longest-waiting dogs, always labeled. The real photos and videos are on every profile.
- The Hire button goes to the dog's APA adoption page. APA's team handles every adoption.

GoodHire is a UCL Live Austin project, not an official Austin Pets Alive! site. No accounts, no tracking. Your first name and recruit stay on your phone.
